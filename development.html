01 — Modular Instructional Media Products

Full-series English and Korean lesson plans & slide decks

To standardize teaching quality across Homat Academy’s visual identity and package standalone educational media products, I authored two structured curriculum libraries:

English Curriculum Suite (All CEFR Levels · Dual Edition)

Complete lesson-plan and presentation library spanning beginner through advanced CEFR tiers. Lessons ship in paired editions—a pen-friendly Interactive Class Edition (warm-up, inductive grammar, authentic listening/speaking tasks, guided writing, and exit tickets) plus a printable Practice & Workbook Edition with drills, error-correction labs, and answer keys.

Korean Curriculum Suite (Beginner → Intermediate 3B)

Structured bilingual slide units (including the Sejong Korean progression through 3B) authored directly in Hangul—without romanization crutches—alongside copy-edited right-to-left Persian, integrating conjugation rules, common-mistake diagnostics, localized dialogues, phonology, idioms, reading, and guided writing.

02 — Platform Product Requirements & QA

Everyday support and enrollment workflows on Homat Academy

As captured on 19 November 2026, Homat Academy hosts 5 published courses encompassing 197 structured lessons and 28 quiz items, serving 47 registered students across 64 total course enrollments (including 9 new enrollments in the 30-day window from 20 October to 19 November 2026). Beyond course content, an educational platform succeeds or fails on everyday learner usability: asking a support question, completing checkout on mobile, and reading bidirectional Persian and foreign-language typography cleanly.

  • Student support ticketing & decoupled LMS scope: While Tutor LMS handles public course syllabus display (with protected lesson delivery governed separately by Homat Academy’s proprietary DRM system), I defined functional requirements, directed WordPress/PHP customization, and tested both the learner ticket-submission view and the administrator reply panel.
  • Enrollment & readability improvements: Specified and verified WooCommerce checkout simplifications, clearer course-page presentation, and self-hosted Persian web fonts for reliable mobile loading.
