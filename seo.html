A clearer path from search to learning.

A hands-on SEO and content project on my own WordPress language-learning platform.

PROJECT NOTESHomat AcademyDiscuss a project ↗

The context

02

What I did

Reviewed Search Console data, mapped redirects from legacy product URLs to course pages, improved content and search-intent alignment, and examined indexation patterns.

What changed
