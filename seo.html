01 — Venture & Structural Context

When commerce plugins compete with editorial structure

On small independent learning platforms, software defaults frequently dictate information architecture. Coupling WooCommerce (checkout and enrollment products) with Tutor LMS (course catalogs) created overlapping product and course URLs targeting identical learner queries, splitting authority and confusing both readers and search crawlers.

At the same time, educational articles and course landing pages needed clearer differentiation between informational language-learning queries and course-enrollment intent.

02 — Governance & Editorial Interventions

Aligning technical hygiene with reader intent

  • URL consolidation & redirect mapping: Audited legacy WooCommerce product pages against canonical Tutor LMS course destinations and implemented structured redirects to present a single authoritative destination per course.
  • Search-intent editorial revision: Reviewed query patterns in Google Search Console to rewrite headings, course descriptions, and supporting articles around concrete questions asked by prospective learners.
  • Indexation monitoring: Examined crawl and indexation reports continuously to identify thin, duplicate, or orphaned pages and prioritize editorial effort where audience demand already existed.
